# FluxGrid Google Play Copy - English

## App name

FluxGrid: Block Puzzle

## Short description

Place pieces, clear lines, and survive evolving tier challenges.

## Full description

FluxGrid is a portrait 2D block puzzle where every move can reshape your next decision.

Drag one of three pieces onto the 10x10 board. Complete rows and columns, open new space, and build your combo. When gravity activates, blocks fall and the board changes, turning a strong clear into a new tactical opportunity.

ENDLESS MODE

As your score rises, the tier system changes the risks on the board. Break ice, contain spreading virus blocks, use bombs at the right moment, and make room for larger pieces as the run becomes more demanding. Your Endless run is saved on your device so you can continue later when eligible.

TIMED MODE

Score as much as possible before time runs out. Strong clears add time, so choosing the right move matters as much as moving quickly.

FEATURES

• Responsive drag-and-drop 2D play
• Row and column clears, gravity, and chain opportunities
• Evolving Endless tiers and board risks
• Special ice, virus, and bomb blocks
• Combos, achievements, and detailed statistics
• Turkish and English language support
• Adjustable sound, music, and haptics

FluxGrid is supported by ads. No in-app purchase or user account is required. Core progress and settings are stored on your device; internet access may be used for ads, diagnostics, and optional notifications.
